A learned super-resolution CNN fuses the sparse field with a high-resolution land-use covariate to recover detail a smear cannot. One map toggle: stations-only ↔ dense 1 km.

Skill is measured against bilinear interpolation on held-out fields; real held-out-station RMSE trains on Kaggle with Earth-Engine AOD × CPCB. Positive skill only when the model truly adds sub-grid signal.
