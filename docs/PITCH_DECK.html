Innovation judged 25%

Hybrid per-km² attribution
GBM + SHAP blended with chemical-signature priors — and it abstains: no ML blame without out-of-sample skill (R² gate), no blame from unobserved markers (coverage mask)
Forecast-triggered GRAP
our 24h forecast maps straight onto the statutory CAQM GRAP stage bands — the graded response fires a day before observed AQI would trigger it
Multi-agent operational loop
six agents on one graph with a spike gate and per-node latency stamps — the full signal-to-action chain is audited, replayable, and demoed live
Prescriptive, not just predictive
cited counterfactual what-if + an optimiser that ranks intervention packages by ΔAQI × people protected per inspector-hour

Also: compound multi-hazard alerts (heat×smog by IMD × CPCB criteria; dust×traffic co-occurrence from live attribution) · multimodal RAG dossiers whose schema, API and UI carry both regulation text and Sentinel-2 patches (CV detections re-populate on the next inference run)

What-if simulator + optimiser

Pick an intervention — odd-even, C&D ban, a full GRAP Stage III package — and run the counterfactual over attribution × forecast. Read the ΔAQI, people protected, ₹, and CO₂e cards, each with its citations. Then let the optimiser rank whole packages by impact per inspector-hour, live behind /optimize.

Intervention magnitudes are literature-cited (Delhi odd-even ≈4–7% ambient; CAQM GRAP stage schedules) — not invented sliders. Engine: E3 counterfactual with dispersion coupling + E7 impact layered on.

A learned super-resolution CNN fuses the sparse field with a high-resolution land-use covariate to recover detail a smear cannot. One map toggle: stations-only ↔ dense 1 km.

Skill measured against bilinear interpolation on held-out synthetic fields, and labeled exactly that; real held-out-station training on Kaggle GPU with Earth-Engine AOD × CPCB is the stated next step.

Honest by construction

Attribution abstains — no skill, no blame: cities failing the R² gate fall back to cited signature priors, and marker-less cells shrink to the city mean
SHAP drivers per cell show why a source was blamed, in µg/m³, with the model's own R² on display
Enforcement never over-claims — category-level citations, <2% contributors never enter the worklist, notices are drafts for officer review
Nothing fabricated reaches production — impact figures return null over faked constants, and demo fixtures are labeled as fixtures
