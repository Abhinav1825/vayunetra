GRAP — GRADED RESPONSE ACTION PLAN FOR NCR, STAGE-WISE ACTIONS (SUMMARY)

Authority: CAQM (Commission for Air Quality Management in NCR and Adjoining Areas).
The revised GRAP is invoked on Delhi's AQI forecast (IMD/IITM), acting PRE-EMPTIVELY
on forecasts rather than after exceedance — the same forecast-then-act philosophy
this platform implements.

Stage I — "Poor" (AQI 201-300):
  dust control enforcement at C&D sites, mechanised sweeping and water sprinkling,
  strict PUC enforcement, no open burning of waste/biomass (fines).

Stage II — "Very Poor" (AQI 301-400):
  intensified inspections; regulated DG-set use per the fuel directions; parking fees
  raised to discourage private transport; augmented bus/metro services.

Stage III — "Severe" (AQI 401-450):
  BAN on construction & demolition (with exemptions for railways, metro, airports,
  hospitals, defence and linear public projects); closure of stone crushers and
  mining; restrictions on BS-III petrol / BS-IV diesel light motor vehicles in
  identified districts; brick kilns not on approved fuels closed.

Stage IV — "Severe+" (AQI > 450):
  entry of truck traffic into Delhi restricted (essentials/CNG/electric exempt);
  ban on Delhi-registered diesel MGVs/HGVs except essentials; industries not on
  approved cleaner fuels close even if otherwise compliant; C&D ban extends to
  linear public projects; public/private offices to 50% strength advisories;
  schools may shift online as directed.

Relationship to NCAP: NCAP is the national programme setting multi-year PM
reduction targets (city action plans, ~40% reduction ambition by 2026 vs 2017);
GRAP is the NCR-specific emergency-response regulation. They are complementary:
NCAP plans, GRAP reacts — and both depend on knowing sources and forecasts.
