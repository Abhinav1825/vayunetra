Graded Response Action Plan (GRAP) — India
Source: Commission for Air Quality Management in National Capital Region and Adjoining Areas (CAQM)
Document ID: GRAP-2023-CONSOLIDATED

=============================================================
GRADED RESPONSE ACTION PLAN (GRAP) — FULL TEXT
=============================================================

GRAP is a set of emergency measures activated as per Air Quality Index (AQI) levels in the Delhi-NCR region. These measures are graded based on the severity of pollution, and each stage of GRAP has specific restrictions and requirements.

=============================================================
STAGE 1 — AQI 201–300 (POOR)
=============================================================

1.1 Dust Control at Construction Sites
- All construction sites must register on the CAQM portal and comply with dust suppression norms.
- Anti-smog guns mandatory for sites exceeding 500 sq.m.
- Regular sprinkling of water on roads and construction areas.
- Storage of construction material in covered areas only.
- Proper green net coverage of construction structures.
- Violation penalty: ₹5,000 to ₹1,00,000 per instance.

1.2 Road Dust Control
- Mechanical sweeping of all national highways, expressways, state highways within city limits.
- Water sprinkling on roads through tankers twice daily.
- Proper disposal of waste and debris from road construction/maintenance activities.

1.3 Vehicular Control
- Enhanced checking of Pollution Under Control (PUC) certificates by traffic police.
- Strict action against vehicles emitting visible smoke.
- Removal of old/end-of-life vehicles from roads.

1.4 Industrial/Generator Control
- Hotels and restaurants: switch to approved fuels (LPG, PNG, approved bio-fuel).
- No use of coal, firewood, biomass in hotels and restaurants.
- Industrial units to maintain emission log and share with SPCB.

1.5 Open Burning
- Complete ban on open burning of waste, leaves, garbage.
- Real-time fire monitoring via satellite feed; immediate action on detected fires.
- Fine: ₹5,000 per incident of open burning.

=============================================================
STAGE 2 — AQI 301–400 (VERY POOR)
=============================================================

(All Stage 1 measures continue, plus:)

2.1 Construction
- Demolition activities of all types banned.
- Stone crushing banned.
- Only essential construction activities permitted (hospitals, roads, metro, defence).

2.2 Diesel Generators
- DG sets banned (except for essential services: hospitals, railways, telecom towers).
- Mandatory DG declaration by industries and commercial establishments.

2.3 Vehicles
- Strict enforcement against heavy diesel trucks entering city limits during day hours.
- Light commercial vehicles (BS-IV and below): restricted entry.
- Fly-ash transport only in covered vehicles.

2.4 Industries
- Industries running on pet coke banned.
- All units to maximize use of PNG; coal usage minimized.
- Random inspections by SPCB teams with 24-hour enforcement.

=============================================================
STAGE 3 — AQI 401–450 (SEVERE)
=============================================================

(All Stage 1, 2 measures continue, plus:)

3.1 Construction
- All construction activities banned except those with essential/emergency exemptions.
- Penalty for violations: ₹50,000 per incident + sealing of site.

3.2 Vehicles
- Odd-even vehicle scheme for private cars in affected districts.
- Diesel trucks banned; only CNG/electric trucks permitted.
- Increased deployment of DRTC/DTC buses with reduced fares.

3.3 Schools and Offices
- Schools (up to Class 5) to shift to online/hybrid mode.
- Government departments encouraged to grant 50% WFH.

3.4 Brick Kilns and Industrial Units
- Brick kilns to shut down operations.
- Industries with high pollution load directed to reduce production to 50%.
- Hot mix plants and stone crushers: operations halted.

=============================================================
STAGE 4 — AQI >450 (SEVERE+)
=============================================================

(All previous measures continue, plus:)

4.1 Emergency Measures
- All construction and demolition activities: complete halt.
- Factories and industries: major reduction in operations; highly polluting units: shut down.
- Schools (up to Class 12) closed.
- Government offices operate with minimum staff; remaining WFH.
- Odd-even scheme expanded; private car bans possible.
- Emergency emergency response protocol: coordination with NDMA.

4.2 Emergency Health Response
- State governments to establish emergency health coordination.
- Civil hospitals: additional respiratory care wards activated.
- Helpline numbers published; mobile health units deployed.

=============================================================
LEGAL BASIS FOR ENFORCEMENT
=============================================================

1. Environment (Protection) Act, 1986 — Section 5 directions for closure/prohibition.
2. Air (Prevention and Control of Pollution) Act, 1981 — SPCB enforcement authority.
3. National Green Tribunal Act, 2010 — NGT directions have been used to enforce GRAP.
4. Motor Vehicles Act, 2019 — Traffic penalties for vehicular pollution violations.
5. Solid Waste Management Rules, 2016 — Prohibition on open burning of waste.
6. Construction and Demolition Waste Management Rules, 2016 — Construction site norms.

=============================================================
INSPECTION CHECKLIST FOR FIELD INSPECTORS
=============================================================

Construction Site Inspection:
[ ] Valid registration on CAQM portal (website/QR code visible at site)
[ ] Anti-smog gun/water sprinkler operational and in use
[ ] Green net coverage of all exposed structures (≥70% coverage)
[ ] Construction material stored in covered/enclosed areas
[ ] Site boundary has dust suppression barriers
[ ] Record of daily water sprinkling maintained at site
[ ] Concrete mixing done only in enclosed/covered areas
[ ] No open burning of construction waste on site

Industrial Unit Inspection:
[ ] Valid Consent-to-Operate (CTO) from SPCB visible at premises
[ ] Emission monitoring data log available and updated
[ ] Stack emission within prescribed limits (last test report)
[ ] DG set type documented; if diesel, compliance with ban period
[ ] Approved fuel in use (CTO-specified); coal/pet coke banned during GRAP 2+
[ ] No unauthorized discharge to air/water

Open Burning Inspection:
[ ] Check for any smoldering waste/garbage/leaves within 500m radius
[ ] Photograph and GPS-tag any burning instances
[ ] Issue on-the-spot notice with fine of ₹5,000
[ ] Report to control room for satellite confirmation
