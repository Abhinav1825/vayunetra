CPCB AQI Health Breakpoints and Standard Operating Procedures
Source: Central Pollution Control Board, India
Document ID: CPCB-AQI-BREAKPOINTS-2024

=============================================================
1. NATIONAL AMBIENT AIR QUALITY STANDARDS (NAAQS) INDIA
=============================================================

The National Ambient Air Quality Standards (NAAQS) define the maximum allowable concentrations of key air pollutants over specific averaging periods.

Pollutant              | Annual Average | 24-hour Average | Short-term (1-hour)
-----------------------|---------------|-----------------|--------------------
PM2.5 (µg/m³)          | 40            | 60              | —
PM10 (µg/m³)           | 60            | 100             | —
NO2 (µg/m³)            | 40            | 80              | —
SO2 (µg/m³)            | 50            | 80              | —
CO (mg/m³)             | 2             | 4               | 10 (8-hr)
O3 (µg/m³)             | —             | 100             | 180 (1-hr)

2. CPCB AQI CATEGORIES AND HEALTH IMPLICATIONS
=============================================================

AQI Range   | Category      | Health Implications
------------|---------------|----------------------------------------------
0–50        | Good          | Minimal impact; air quality considered satisfactory
51–100      | Satisfactory  | Minor breathing discomfort to sensitive people
101–200     | Moderate      | Breathing discomfort to people with lung disease (asthma); discomfort to general public with prolonged exposure
201–300     | Poor          | Breathing discomfort to most people on prolonged exposure
301–400     | Very Poor     | Respiratory illness on prolonged exposure; Heart disease patients may experience significant health impacts
401–500     | Severe        | Health impacts even on light physical activity; serious impact on people with heart/lung disease
>500        | Severe+       | Emergency health conditions; entire population likely to be affected

=============================================================
3. HEALTH ADVISORIES BY SEGMENT AND AQI LEVEL
=============================================================

3.1 General Population
- AQI 0–100: Normal activities; no restrictions advised.
- AQI 101–200: Reduce prolonged outdoor exertion; wear masks if outdoors for extended periods.
- AQI 201–300: Avoid prolonged outdoor activities; wear N95 masks; use air purifiers indoors.
- AQI 301+: Stay indoors; keep windows closed; use air purifiers; seek medical attention if symptoms appear.

3.2 Sensitive Groups (Children, Elderly, Pregnant Women, Respiratory Patients)
- AQI 51–100: Take extra caution; limit time outdoors.
- AQI 101–200: Avoid outdoor activities; use masks; keep medication accessible.
- AQI 201+: Stay indoors; seek medical attention immediately if experiencing symptoms.

3.3 Outdoor Workers
- AQI 101–200: Shift heavy outdoor work to early morning or late evening when AQI may be lower; use masks.
- AQI 201–300: Reduce workload; take frequent breaks indoors; wear protective masks.
- AQI 301+: Halt outdoor work if possible; use respirators; consider rest assignments indoors.

3.4 Schools and Educational Institutions
- AQI 201–300: Cancel outdoor sports and activities; keep children indoors during recess.
- AQI 301+: Consider closing schools or shifting to online learning; all outdoor activities cancelled.

4. EMERGENCY RESPONSE TRIGGERS (GRAP / NCAP)
=============================================================

Level 1 — AQI 201–300 (Poor)
Trigger: City AQI crosses 200 for 3 consecutive days.
Actions:
- Increase mechanized road sweeping frequency to 2x daily on main roads.
- Ban open burning of garbage and agricultural waste within city limits.
- Deploy water sprinklers at construction sites generating dust.
- Enhanced enforcement at known pollution hotspots (industrial clusters, construction sites).
- Issue public advisory via SMS broadcast and social media.

Level 2 — AQI 301–400 (Very Poor)
Trigger: City AQI crosses 300.
Actions:
- All Level 1 actions continue.
- Ban operation of diesel generators except hospitals and emergency services.
- Restrict movement of older vehicles (pre-BS-IV) during peak hours (8 AM–6 PM).
- Increase frequency of anti-dust campaigns at major construction sites.
- Mandatory anti-smog guns at construction sites exceeding 20,000 sq.m.

Level 3 — AQI 401–450 (Severe)
Trigger: City AQI crosses 400.
Actions:
- All Level 1 and 2 actions continue.
- Odd-Even vehicle scheme implementation.
- Closure of brick kilns and hot mix plants within 30km radius.
- Halt all non-essential construction activities.
- Close schools for outdoor activities; advise work-from-home.
- Emergency response teams deployed to conduct real-time source verification.

Level 4 — AQI >450 (Severe+)
Trigger: City AQI crosses 450.
Actions:
- All Level 1, 2, 3 actions continue.
- Complete vehicle ban (except emergency and public transport).
- All construction projects halted including essential.
- Industries directed to reduce production by 50%; highly polluting units shut down.
- Educational institutions closed; government offices encourage WFH.
- Emergency health camps set up at multiple locations.
- Direct coordination with hospitals to prepare for increased respiratory admissions.
