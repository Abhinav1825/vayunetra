CAQM STATUTORY DIRECTIONS — DUST CONTROL AT CONSTRUCTION & DEMOLITION SITES (SUMMARY)

Authority: Commission for Air Quality Management in NCR and Adjoining Areas (CAQM),
constituted under the CAQM Act 2021. This document summarises the dust-mitigation
directions applicable to construction and demolition (C&D) projects in NCR
(reference frame: CAQM Direction No. 11 of 2021 and subsequent consolidations).

Key obligations for C&D sites:
1. Anti-smog guns: projects above the notified built-up-area thresholds must deploy
   anti-smog guns proportionate to construction area, operational during working hours.
2. Water sprinkling on haul roads, excavation faces and material handling points;
   mechanised sweeping of paved access roads.
3. Wind-breaking perimeter barriers of prescribed height; green netting / covering
   of scaffolding and buildings under construction.
4. Covered storage and transport: construction material and C&D waste must be stored
   under cover and transported in covered vehicles; wheel-washing at site exits.
5. Registration and self-audit of large projects on the CAQM/state web portals with
   remote-monitoring (video fencing, PM sensors) for the largest category.
6. Non-compliance attracts closure of the site and environmental compensation under
   the polluter-pays principle; repeat violations escalate to sealing.

Applicability: NCT of Delhi and NCR districts of Haryana, Rajasthan and Uttar Pradesh.
Relationship to GRAP: these obligations apply at all times; GRAP Stages III/IV
additionally BAN C&D activity (with exemptions for railways, metro, airports,
hospitals and linear public projects) when AQI crosses the Severe thresholds.
