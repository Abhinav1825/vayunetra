CPCB Dust Control Norms for Construction Sites — 2016
Source: Central Pollution Control Board
Document ID: CPCB-DUST-NORMS-2016

=============================================================
DUST CONTROL NORMS FOR CONSTRUCTION SITES — INDIA
=============================================================

These norms are applicable to all construction, demolition, and renovation activities in India, particularly in non-attainment cities and areas covered under GRAP.

Legal Authority: Environment (Protection) Act, 1986; Air (Prevention and Control of Pollution) Act, 1981.

=============================================================
PART 1 — APPLICABILITY AND REGISTRATION
=============================================================

1.1 All construction projects with a built-up area ≥5,000 sq.m or involving land disturbance ≥500 sq.m must:
(a) Obtain prior Environmental Clearance (EC) or register with the State SPCB.
(b) Prepare and submit a Dust Management Plan (DMP) before commencement.
(c) Appoint a Site Pollution Control Officer (SPCO) responsible for compliance.

1.2 Registration Requirements:
- Register on CAQM portal (for Delhi-NCR) or State portal within 7 days of commencement.
- Display registration number prominently at site entrance.
- Provide contact details of SPCO and owner; accessible 24x7 for regulatory inspection.

=============================================================
PART 2 — MANDATORY DUST SUPPRESSION MEASURES
=============================================================

2.1 Water Sprinkling (Mandatory)
- Water must be sprinkled at least TWICE daily at:
  (a) All active excavation and earthwork areas.
  (b) Site access/exit points (to trap dust from vehicles).
  (c) Stockpile areas of excavated soil, sand, aggregate.
  (d) Perimeter areas of the site.
- During GRAP Stage 1+: increase frequency to 4 times daily.
- Automated water sprinkler systems recommended for sites >20,000 sq.m.

2.2 Green/Anti-Dust Netting
- All vertical surfaces (walls, hoardings, scaffolding): minimum 70% coverage with green net/tarpaulin.
- Material stockpiles: covered with tarpaulin or dust suppression sheets at all times.
- All trucks exiting site must be covered with tarpaulin.

2.3 Anti-Smog Guns
- Mandatory for sites ≥20,000 sq.m.
- Must be operational during all working hours.
- Coverage radius: minimum 50m; deployed at site periphery.
- Minimum 2 guns per 20,000 sq.m of active construction area.

2.4 Enclosure of Operations
- Concrete batching and mixing: only in enclosed, covered structures.
- Cutting, grinding, sanding of materials: done in enclosed areas with dust collectors.
- Waste material: stored in covered bins; not left in open piles.

2.5 Construction Material Storage
- Sand, soil, aggregate: stored in enclosed/covered bunkers or covered with tarpaulin.
- Bagged cement: stored in covered, waterproof areas.
- No stockpiling material within 5m of public roads/footpaths.

=============================================================
PART 3 — VEHICLE AND TRANSPORT REQUIREMENTS
=============================================================

3.1 All vehicles entering and exiting the construction site must:
- Have valid Pollution Under Control (PUC) certificate.
- Be covered with tarpaulin when carrying loose material (soil, sand, aggregate, debris).
- Carry vehicle registration number clearly visible; comply with weight limits.

3.2 Vehicle Wheels:
- Wheel wash/rumble strips mandatory at all site exits to remove mud and dust.
- Vehicles must not track mud/dust onto public roads; sweeping of exit roads mandatory.

3.3 Idling:
- No vehicle to idle at site for more than 3 minutes; engines off when waiting.

=============================================================
PART 4 — DEMOLITION ACTIVITIES
=============================================================

4.1 Pre-demolition requirements:
- Site boundary fencing: minimum 1.8m height, dust-proof sheeting.
- Cover all windows and openings of adjacent buildings.
- Notify adjacent residents/businesses 72 hours in advance.
- Wet-wrecking method preferred over dry demolition.

4.2 During demolition:
- Continuous water misting during all demolition activities.
- No dropping of materials from height >2m without dust containment chute.
- Immediate bagging and removal of demolition waste.

4.3 Post-demolition:
- Site stabilization within 72 hours.
- Temporary vegetation cover or dust suppressant on bare earth.

=============================================================
PART 5 — MONITORING AND RECORD-KEEPING
=============================================================

5.1 Daily records to be maintained:
- Water consumption log (quantity sprinkled; frequency per day).
- Anti-smog gun operating hours.
- Vehicle movement log (in/out, PUC status).
- Any incidents of dust escape with corrective actions taken.

5.2 Monthly compliance report to be submitted to SPCB.

5.3 CPCB/SPCB officers have the right to inspect sites at any time without prior notice.

=============================================================
PART 6 — PENALTIES FOR NON-COMPLIANCE
=============================================================

Violation                                         | Penalty
--------------------------------------------------|-------------------------------
No registration / no DMP                          | ₹5,000–1,00,000 + stop work
No water sprinkling (during active construction)  | ₹5,000 per inspection
No green net coverage                             | ₹5,000–50,000
No anti-smog gun (mandatory sites)                | ₹10,000–50,000
Vehicle without tarpaulin / expired PUC           | ₹5,000 per vehicle
Open burning of C&D waste                         | ₹5,000–1,00,000 + FIR
Non-compliance after notice                       | Site sealing + ₹1,00,000

=============================================================
PART 7 — EXEMPTIONS
=============================================================

The following activities are exempt from certain provisions:
- Emergency repairs to essential infrastructure (power, water, sewage).
- Government-declared emergency projects (national security, disaster relief).
- Agriculture activities on rural land (different rules apply).

Note: Exemptions must be documented and certified by relevant authority.
