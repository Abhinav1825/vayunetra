CAQM DIRECTIONS — APPROVED FUELS FOR INDUSTRY IN NCR (SUMMARY)

Authority: Commission for Air Quality Management in NCR and Adjoining Areas (CAQM).
Reference frame: CAQM standard list of approved fuels for NCR and the directions on
industrial fuel switching (2022 consolidation; earlier Direction No. 47/2022 line).

Key provisions:
1. Standard fuel list: only fuels on the CAQM-approved list may be used in NCR —
   PNG/CNG/LNG, LPG, electricity, biogas/CBG, low-sulphur diesel for DG sets within
   permitted use, and specified biomass pellets for identified applications.
2. Coal ban: use of coal (including low-sulphur imported coal) is prohibited across
   NCR for industrial, domestic and miscellaneous applications, with a carve-out for
   thermal power plants and industries using coal with emission-control per CPCB norms
   outside the ban's scope.
3. Fuel switching: industries in NCR industrial areas with PNG supply infrastructure
   must operate on PNG or approved cleaner fuels; unconnected areas were given
   switch-over timelines, after which non-compliant units face closure.
4. GRAP linkage: under GRAP Stage IV, industries not running on approved cleaner
   fuels must close (exemptions: milk/dairy and life-saving/medical manufacturing).
5. DG sets: diesel generator use is regulated by capacity and emission retrofits
   (RECD), with graded restrictions tightening through GRAP stages.

Enforcement: state pollution control boards (DPCC/HSPCB/RSPCB/UPPCB) inspect and
report compliance to CAQM; violations attract closure directions and environmental
compensation.
